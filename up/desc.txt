A 12-week Pilates and yoga program in one Google Sheet. Every session is already planned and dated, with a free follow-along video ready to play. Tap a video, follow along, tick the box, and the sheet does the rest. Each session fits on 2 rows, and one tap jumps you to your next session or any week, so there's no endless scrolling.

You get a PDF with links to your own Google Sheet. Works in the free Google Sheets app on phone, tablet or computer. Not for Excel or Numbers, and not a printable.

4 sessions a week for 12 weeks: 2 Pilates sessions for strength and core, and 2 yoga sessions for flexibility and recovery. 3 phases (Foundation, Build and Strong), each one a little harder. All you need is a mat.
